FAQ


How does GhostCart automate order fulfillment for my eBay store?
GhostCart automates order fulfillment by processing thousands of your eBay orders directly through your Amazon account, or you can leverage GhostCart's Amazon Managed accounts, or even a mix of both (hybryd mode).

How do I fulfill auto ordering from Amazon with GhostCart?
GhostCart makes it extremely easy for you to fulfill orders from Amazon. Simply load balance from Paypal or Payoneer, and we'll do the rest for you.

How do I process orders with GhostCart?
With GhostCart, you process orders automatically. Our job is to make it effortless for you to fulfill thousands of orders and quickly deliver them to your customers. Auto-Tracking and Auto-Messaging make it possible for you and your customers to know where orders are at all times.

Can GhostCart help me resolve buyer account limitations with my sources?
Yes. GhostCart can fulfill your orders, so you can enjoy better buyer account and credit card limits, tax exemptions, and other benefits.

What ecommerce fulfillment services does GhostCart offer?
GhostCart offers complete ecommerce fulfillment solutions, including managed Amazon accounts (US & UK), hybrid auto-ordering, AliExpress sourcing, automated tracking, and returns management - all designed to scale your online business without the manual work.

What are Managed Amazon Accounts?
 GhostCart fulfills using vetted Amazon accounts (US & UK), so you can scale without risking your own accounts.

Can I still fulfill manually?
Yes. and even if you fulfill yourself, GhostCart auto-converts shipments into valid carrier tracking.

Does GhostCart support AliExpress?
Yes. You can source globally from AliExpress, with automation covering orders and tracking.

How does auto-tracking for manual orders work?
Even when you fulfill orders yourself, GhostCart converts your shipments into valid carrier-recognized tracking numbers that sync back to your store.

Can I switch between fulfillment modes?
Yes, you have granular control and can switch between Managed Amazon, Auto Ordering from your own Amazon accounts, Hybrid or manual with auto-tracking at any time.

How does GhostCart handle returns and refunds?
The system automatically processes returns and refunds above the minimum price as per our fulfillment policy as part of the complete order management, not just the initial fulfillment.

What makes this different from rigid automation models?
Unlike single-option solutions, GhostCart offers "one stack, multiple options" - you can use Managed Accounts / Auto Ordering from your Accounts or Hybrid, all in one platform.

How does automatically fulfill the order’s line items work?
GhostCart processes orders automatically through your chosen fulfillment mode - whether Managed Amazon accounts, your own accounts, or Hybrid mode.

What does automatically fulfill the order’s line items mean?
This means the system handles order processing automatically without manual intervention, including placing orders and updating tracking.

Can I automatically fulfill orders on dropshipping stores?
Yes, GhostCart handles automatic order fulfillment for eBay and other supported platforms with policy-aligned sourcing and carrier-recognized tracking.


What aspects of order processing does GhostCart handle automatically?
GhostCart provides comprehensive automation, including supplier stock monitoring, auto-repricing, and product tracking. It also ensures next-to-real-time price and stock synchronization, order fulfillment, and timely tracking updates.


What do I need to do to ensure smooth order processing with GhostCart?
To ensure your orders are processed smoothly, you need to add your Amazon buyer accounts and keep enough GhostCart Auto-Order (AO) credits topped up at all times for Auto Ordering from your own Amazon account, and make sure to load enough balance in $ if your are using our Managed accounts or the hybrid mode.
