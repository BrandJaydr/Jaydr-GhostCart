## Change Reviewer pass 3 (fix-needed) — applied
- `GET` no longer holds a pooled client across the outbound eBay HTTP call:
  state verification is one short `withTenant` transaction, the 401 is returned
  after it commits/releases, and `exchangeCodeForToken` runs with no
  transaction open. This removes the pool-starvation regression (pool max 10,
  connectionTimeoutMillis 5000).
- Deleted the dead `catch`-branch string match on `Invalid or expired state
  parameter` and the dead `row.stored_state !== state` check (and its unused
  SELECT column).

  plainly that the mocked suite cannot see RLS or column-list errors.
- Documented the `audit_events` append-only growth risk and the cleanup
  follow-up at the INSERT site; no cleanup job was added (separate job, Medium).
