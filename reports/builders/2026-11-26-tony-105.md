## What changed
- `src/worker/aliexpress-token-refresh.ts` (new): scheduled sweep that selects
  due AliExpress connections (expired or within 5-min skew, disabled excluded),
  rotates each token through the GC-202 `AliExpressTokenProvider`, and persists
  the rotated pair. Per-connection failures raise one critical alert event
  (`aliexpress.token_refresh_failed`) and are skipped, so one bad connection
  cannot crash the worker or spin a retry loop. No token/credential material
  is written to logs or alert payloads.
- `src/worker/index.ts`: register the refresh tick (10-min interval) and clear
  it on SIGTERM alongside the existing scheduler timer.
- `src/__tests__/worker/aliexpress-token-refresh.test.ts` (new): proves (a)
  expired-token state triggers refresh and persists the rotated pair, (b) a
  refresh failure raises an alert and resolves without throwing, and (c) a
  failed connection is contained so the rest of the sweep still runs.
